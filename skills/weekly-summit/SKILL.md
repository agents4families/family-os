# Weekly Summit

## Purpose
One shared picture of the week, agreed by both adults, so the weekdays run on autopilot. This is the highest-value ritual in the system.

## Autonomy
A1. Output is a plan the adults accept; actions follow separately.

## Context to load
- `context/routines.md`, `context/charter.md`, `context/calendar-rules.md`
- Live: calendar for the next 8 days; last week's `outputs/weekly/` plan and any carry-overs in `tasks.json`
- `harness/state/inbox.md`; last meal plan if present

## Steps
1. **Look back (2 min):** what from last week's plan didn't happen? Carry over or drop each item.
2. **Calendar pass:** list the week by day. Flag conflicts, double-bookings, unassigned pickups, travel days, late nights over the max in `calendar-rules.md`.
3. **Load check:** count evenings out and heavy days per adult. If either adult exceeds the protected-time rules, say so plainly and offer two trades.
4. **Prep pass:** for each event, what must be ready (forms, gifts, gear, babysitter, reservations)? Route big events to `event-prep`.
5. **Meals:** run `meal-plan` for the week using the calendar's real constraints.
6. **Money touchpoints:** bills due, subscriptions renewing, birthdays needing gifts.
7. **Home:** one maintenance or tidy-up item at most (from `home-maintenance`).
8. **Inbox sweep:** decide each parked item: schedule, delegate, drop.
9. **Top 3:** the three things that make this a good week. Include one for connection (a date night, a kid one-on-one, a family activity).
10. **Assign owners and due dates.** Write to `tasks.json`.

## Output contract
One page, saved to `outputs/weekly/YYYY-Www-plan.md`:

```
# Week of Oct 19
Top 3: 1) Ship the science project Wed. 2) Date night Fri. 3) Pre-book Thanksgiving travel.
Watch-outs: Tue 4:30 double-booked (soccer / dentist). Thu Parent A out of town.
Day by day (who drives, key events, prep)
Meals: Mon tacos · Tue sheet-pan chicken (GF) · …
Money: Electric due Thu · Streaming renews Sat ($15.99) → keep?
Tasks assigned (with owners and dates)
Decisions needed from both of you (max 3)
```

## Guardrails
- Keep the summit short. Park anything that needs more than five minutes to the decision-brief queue.
- Don't assign tasks to a person without a visible reason from the charter or calendar.
- When the adults disagree on a trade, present both options with costs; don't choose.

## Done when
- Every day has a named driver for every handoff.
- Every task has an owner and a date.
- Both adults have seen the plan, and any approvals are recorded.
