# Evening Reset

## Purpose
Tomorrow-you shouldn't be surprised. Ten minutes of prep at night removes most of the morning scramble.

## Autonomy
A1. May set reminders at A2 if approved in the ladder.

## Context to load
- `context/routines.md`
- Live: tomorrow's calendar, `tasks.json`, `inbox.md`, tonight's dinner/lunch plan from `outputs/meal-plans/` if present

## Steps
1. Show tomorrow in one glance (first commitment, who drives, weather).
2. List "do tonight" items: lunches, forms to sign, bags, charge devices, laundry for events, thaw dinner.
3. Process `inbox.md` quickly: route each item or park it with a date.
4. Ask: "Anything from today that didn't get done and still matters?" Carry it to a date or drop it.
5. Offer tomorrow's one-line plan for each adult.

## Output contract
A checklist of 5-8 items, each with an owner. Under 150 words.

## Guardrails
- No new big topics at night. Park them in the inbox for the `weekly-summit`.
- Respect the adult wind-down time in `routines.md`.

## Done when
Tomorrow has a named first commitment, an owner for every handoff, and an empty "do tonight" list or a plan for each item.
