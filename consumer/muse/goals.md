# Muse Goal Prompts

Prepend `shared/injection-shield.txt` to any goal that reads mail or browses. Replace `[ ]`.

**1. Dinner reservation**
> Find a dinner for [number] on [date] around [time], [cuisine/vibe], under $[budget] per person, kid-friendly, with good gluten-free handling. Give me three options with availability. Do not book anything until I reply "book [name] at [time] for [number]". After booking, give me the confirmation number and add it to my calendar.

**2. Event tickets**
> Find tickets for [event] on [date] for [number] people. Compare the official seller with resale and tell me the total with fees. Budget $[X]. Do not buy until I approve the exact seats and total. Use a one-time card.

**3. Birthday planning (batch)**
> Plan [child]'s birthday on [date] for about [number] guests, budget $[X]. Propose venue options, a timeline, a food plan (one guest is gluten-free, check for nut allergies [yes/no]), and a draft invitation message. Do not contact anyone, book, or buy until I approve the plan. When approved, list each action you'll take and wait for my yes on each one that costs money or sends a message.

**4. Quote comparison**
> I need [service] at [home area]. Find three well-reviewed providers, summarize reviews and typical price ranges, and draft a request-for-quote message for each. Don't send anything. Tell me what to ask on a phone call.

**5. Price watch**
> Watch [product/trip] and tell me if it drops below $[X] or if availability changes. Check once a day. Notify only when it hits. Do not buy.

**6. Not allowed on Muse (stays in the Family OS ladder at A1)**
School form submission, medical or insurance admin, bill payment, anything involving children's personal records, smart-home locks, and any money move without an exact-amount approval.
