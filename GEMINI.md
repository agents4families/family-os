# Gemini notes

Read and follow `AGENTS.md` in this folder. It is the shared constitution for every AI tool in this household. (If your Gemini CLI version supports it, set `contextFileName` to `AGENTS.md` and delete this file.)

Gemini-specific advantages worth using:
- Native Google Workspace access (Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Keep). Best choice for "what's on the calendar" and shared-document work if the family lives in Google.
- Android and Google Home integration for voice and smart-home actions.

Same rules apply: start read-only, drafts before sends, nothing leaves the household without a human yes.
